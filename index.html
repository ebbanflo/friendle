      HOW TO PLAY

      Everyone gets the same hidden 5-letter word and 6 guesses. A right letter, right spot · B right letter, wrong spot · C not in the word. You see rivals' grids live — colors only, never letters. Solve first for the big points!

      CLASSIC

      Host picks 3 / 5 / 10 words. First solver gets full points (base + speed + fewer-guess bonus), later solvers get a cut. Most points wins.

      LEVEL (Classic & Royale): STD = any word, classic style · EASY / MED / HARD = words from that difficulty band · RAMP = starts easy, ends mean.

      ROYALE ∞

      Everyone starts with 1000 points. Each word, every survivor antes into a pot — the first solver takes it all (unsolved pots roll over and grow). Hit 0 and you're out, spectating. Last player standing wins.

      FRIEND 💗

      One player is the setter and secretly types their own word; everyone else races to guess it. Host picks 1/3/5/10 words each — every player sets that many. Scoring is fewest guesses, not speed, and the setter scores per guesser they stump. The setter watches every grid with the actual letters and flings emoji reactions; solve the word and you join the letter-vision club too. Still-competing guessers only see colors. No shop — it's pure.

      TOWER 🗼

      Co-op, 1–4 players, endless. Stack real 5-letter words into a shared tower — every word must obey the escalating DECREE (use a letter → slotted letters → bans → no vowels…). The host sets DECREE directly: 2–10 words before it escalates. Counterintuitively, LOWER is easier — you cycle to a fresh constraint before the team's vocabulary for the current one runs dry; a high count forces you to keep digging for distinct words under the same brutal rule. Bad words, repeats, decree-breakers and letting the hunger bar empty all cost lives (3 each, capped at 5). Downed? A teammate can buy ✨ Revive (5000) and solve a classic wordle to bring you back with 2 lives — starting a revive pauses the hunger clock for everyone (untimed!) and it resumes fresh once the rescue ends. Every 10th floor the whole team gets a bonus heart (reviving anyone downed), and if the tower's stack happens to spell T-O-W-E-R down a column, that's a bonus heart too. All down = the tower falls; height is glory. Big numbers guaranteed.

      POWER-UP SHOP (Classic & Royale)

      🔍 Peek 75 — see one real letter of a rival's latest guess
      🧊 Freeze 100 — lock rivals' keyboards for 5s
      💡 Hint 75 — reveal a green letter in your grid
      🗑️ Smudge 100 — un-gray a letter on a rival's keyboard
      ⚔️ Duel (stake 0–200) — alternate guesses with a foe on a fresh word; loser pays (Royale: bust = out).
